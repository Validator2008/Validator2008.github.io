What Is This?

Root Access Denied is a musical experiment at the intersection of software engineering and heavy metal — because some problems deserve more than a postmortem.

🤖

AI-Generated

Instruments, vocals, and production are fully AI-generated. The engineering pain behind the lyrics, however, is entirely human.

🎸

Metal Genre

Heavy riffs, anthemic choruses, and the kind of intensity that mirrors a 2am production incident. Therapeutic listening guaranteed.

📋

Real Engineering Themes

Every track tackles a genuine software engineering challenge — tech debt, microservice complexity, scope creep, and organizational dysfunction.

💡

Serious Ideas

Dave's trunk-based development sermon. Charity Majors on observability. The distributed monolith trap. The lyrics are lore, not just noise.
